# **FINBIQ — Product Requirements Document**

**Product:** FINBIQ  
 **Product Type:** AI-powered financial management and fintech platform  
 **Primary Markets:** Individuals and businesses  
 **Product Positioning:** *Your money. Your business. Your financial intelligence.*

---

## **1\. Executive Summary**

FINBIQ is an AI-powered financial management and fintech platform designed to bring everyday financial management, payments, savings, budgeting, investments, financing, and business financial tools into one unified experience.

FINBIQ will allow users to manage their personal and business finances from a single account while receiving personalized financial insights from an AI assistant.

The platform will support two primary experiences:

* **Personal Mode** — for individuals managing income, expenses, savings, budgets, investments, payments, goals, and financing.

* **Business Mode** — for entrepreneurs and businesses managing revenue, expenses, cash flow, payments, budgets, reports, and business financing.

The long-term objective is to become a user's primary financial operating platform rather than simply another payment or banking application.

---

# **2\. Product Vision**

FINBIQ aims to make financial management simpler, more intelligent, and more actionable.

Users should be able to open FINBIQ and immediately understand:

* How much money they have.

* Where their money is going.

* What they need to pay.

* How much they are saving.

* Whether they are staying within budget.

* How close they are to their financial goals.

* What financial actions they should consider next.

The AI assistant should transform financial data into understandable insights and recommendations while keeping users in control of important financial decisions.

### **Vision Statement**

**FINBIQ gives people and businesses one intelligent platform to manage money, build better financial habits, make payments, save, invest, and access financing.**

---

# **3\. Problem Statement**

Individuals and businesses often rely on multiple disconnected platforms for:

* Banking

* Payments

* Budgeting

* Savings

* Investments

* Loans

* Business accounting

* Financial reporting

This fragmentation makes it difficult to obtain a complete picture of financial health.

Users may know their account balance but not understand:

* Why their spending increased.

* Whether they can afford a purchase.

* Whether they are on track to reach a savings goal.

* Where they can reduce expenses.

* Whether their cash flow is healthy.

* What financing options may be appropriate.

Businesses face similar challenges when managing revenue, expenses, cash flow, supplier payments, employee payments, and financing.

**FINBIQ will combine financial infrastructure, financial management, AI intelligence, and financial habit-building into one platform.**

Users should be able to interact with AI through natural language.

 "How much did I spend on food last month?"

 "You spent ₦145,000 on food last month, which was 18% higher than the previous month."

**User:**  
 "How can I reduce it?"

**FINBIQ AI:**  
 "Based on your recent spending, reducing food spending by ₦25,000–₦30,000 per month could be achievable. You could start by reviewing restaurant and delivery expenses."

---

## **15.4 AI Safety Principles**

The AI must:

* Clearly distinguish insights from transactions.

* Never execute important financial actions without user authorization.

* Explain important recommendations.

* Avoid presenting uncertain information as fact.

* Protect sensitive financial information.

* Respect user permissions.

* Maintain auditability for consequential actions.

* Apply appropriate regulatory controls to financial advice.

Users remain responsible for important financial decisions unless FINBIQ is operating through an appropriately authorized financial advisory framework.
